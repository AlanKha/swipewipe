Duplicates

Matched by identical file size and date taken. Click a copy to switch it between keep and bin. Check on your iPhone before deleting lots of these: open Photos → Utilities → Duplicates. If your iPhone shows no duplicates, these copies may be two local files for the same iCloud photo, and deleting one could delete the photo itself. Try one pair first.
